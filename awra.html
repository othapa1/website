What the diagnostic evaluates.

13 questions across four weighted pillars. Process fit leads — because automating the wrong workflow transfers cost rather than removing it.

Process Fit & Automation Leverage · 30%

Which workflows are actually ready to automate?

Evaluates whether target workflows are repetitive, rule-bounded, measurable, and owned — fit criteria that most AI initiatives skip. Surfaces Process-Fit Mismatches, High Automation Debt, and Automation Theater before you invest in architecture.

Security & Tenant Isolation · 25%

Can user data stay isolated under real load?

Evaluates tenant-level data separation across retrieval, tool execution, memory, and generated outputs. Surfaces gaps in prompt injection defenses and sensitive data handling before they become liability events.

Tokenomics & Cost Scalability · 25%

Will your unit economics survive 100× volume?

Evaluates cost attribution granularity, token budgeting per tenant and workflow, and model routing strategy. Identifies Token Gluttony — using flagship models for trivial tasks — before it destroys margins at scale.

Reliability & Production Architecture · 20%

What happens when the model fails or degrades?

Evaluates evaluation harnesses, regression testing, observability coverage, deterministic fallbacks, and incident response playbooks. AI reliability is not uptime — it includes retrieval quality, refusal rates, and policy violations.
